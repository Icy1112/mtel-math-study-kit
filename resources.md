# MTEL Math (63) resources

Every link was checked on 2026-10-28. All are free.

## Official MTEL (free, start here)

- [Field 63 test page](https://www.mtel.nesinc.com/TestView.aspx?f=HTML_FRAG/MA063_TestPage.html): Format, time, passing score, registration.
- [Test objectives (0001–0019)](https://www.mtel.nesinc.com/Content/StudyGuide/MA_SG_obj_63.htm): The official list of what the test covers, with subarea weights. This kit follows it.
- [Full practice test (85 + 2)](https://www.mtel.nesinc.com/content/PracticeTest/MA063_PracticeTest_85.html): The closest thing to the real test. Has an answer key, no explanations. Save it for week 7.
- [Sample multiple-choice questions](https://www.mtel.nesinc.com/Content/StudyGuide/MA_SG_SRI_63.htm): Short official sample with answers.
- [Sample open response + scoring rubric](https://www.mtel.nesinc.com/Content/StudyGuide/MA_SG_CRI_63.asp): Shows what a full open-response task looks like and how it is scored.
- [Formula sheet (PDF)](https://www.mtel.nesinc.com/Content/Docs/MTEL_63_Formulas.pdf): Given on screen during the test. Know what is on it, so you do not memorize it twice.
- [On-screen calculator tutorial (PDF)](https://www.mtel.nesinc.com/Content/Docs/calculator_tutorial.pdf): Practice with this calculator, not a graphing calculator.
- [Test-taking strategies](https://www.mtel.nesinc.com/Content/StudyGuide/MA_SG_strategies_63.asp): Official advice on pacing and guessing.
- [All Field 63 prep materials](https://www.mtel.nesinc.com/TestView.aspx?f=HTML_FRAG/MA063_PrepMaterials.html): Index page for everything above.

## Algebra and functions (Subareas I–II)

- [Khan Academy: Pre-algebra](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/pre-algebra): Fractions, exponents, factors. Use it to fill gaps fast. *(Objectives 0001)*
- [Khan Academy: Algebra 1](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/algebra): Equations, inequalities, lines, quadratics. *(Objectives 0003, 0005)*
- [Khan Academy: Algebra 2](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/algebra2): Polynomials, rational, exponential and log functions. *(Objectives 0003, 0004, 0006, 0007)*
- [Khan Academy: Precalculus](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/precalculus): Complex numbers, matrices, vectors, conics, sequences. *(Objectives 0002, 0004, 0012)*
- [Khan Academy: Trigonometry](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/trigonometry): Unit circle, graphs, identities. *(Objectives 0008)*
- [Khan Academy: Linear algebra](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/linear-algebra): Deeper matrices and vectors, if 0002 is weak. *(Objectives 0002)*
- [Paul's Online Notes: Algebra](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/Classes/Alg/Alg.aspx): Clear written notes with worked examples. Very good for rebuilding. *(Objectives 0001, 0003, 0006, 0007)*
- [OpenStax: Intermediate Algebra 2e (free book)](https://openstax.org/details/books/intermediate-algebra-2e): If you need the slowest, most complete rebuild. *(Objectives 0003, 0005)*
- [OpenStax: College Algebra 2e (free book)](https://openstax.org/details/books/college-algebra-2e): Functions, polynomials, exp and log, systems and matrices. *(Objectives 0002, 0004, 0006, 0007)*
- [OpenStax: Precalculus 2e (free book)](https://openstax.org/details/books/precalculus-2e): Trig, polar form, conics, sequences. *(Objectives 0002, 0008, 0012)*
- [3Blue1Brown: Essence of linear algebra](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/linear-algebra): Short videos that make matrices visual. *(Objectives 0002)*

## Geometry and measurement (Subarea III)

- [Khan Academy: High school geometry](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/geometry): Congruence, similarity, trig, circles, solids, transformations. *(Objectives 0009, 0010, 0011, 0012)*
- [Math Open Reference: Constructions](https://www.mathopenref.com/tocs/constructionstoc.html): Animated compass-and-straightedge constructions. *(Objectives 0010)*
- [GeoGebra Geometry](https://www.geogebra.org/geometry): Draw figures and test theorems yourself. *(Objectives 0010, 0011, 0012)*
- [Math is Fun: Geometry](https://www.mathsisfun.com/geometry/index.html): The simplest explanations. Good first read. *(Objectives 0009, 0010, 0011)*
- [Non-Euclidean geometry overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-Euclidean_geometry): Read the intro and the comparison of the three geometries. *(Objectives 0012)*

## Probability, statistics, calculus, discrete (Subarea IV)

- [Khan Academy: Statistics and probability](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/statistics-probability): Full coverage of 0013 and 0014. *(Objectives 0013, 0014)*
- [Khan Academy: AP Statistics](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/ap-statistics): Confidence intervals and hypothesis tests. *(Objectives 0014)*
- [OpenStax: Introductory Statistics 2e (free book)](https://openstax.org/details/books/introductory-statistics-2e): Reference text for stats. *(Objectives 0013, 0014)*
- [Khan Academy: AP Calculus AB](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/ap-calculus-ab): Limits, derivatives, integrals. The core of 0015–0016. *(Objectives 0015, 0016)*
- [Khan Academy: AP Calculus BC](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/ap-calculus-bc): Integration by parts, arc length, extras. *(Objectives 0016)*
- [Paul's Online Notes: Calculus I](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/Classes/CalcI/CalcI.aspx): Limits, derivatives and their applications, with many worked examples. *(Objectives 0015, 0016)*
- [Paul's Online Notes: Calculus II](https://tutorial.math.lamar.edu/Classes/CalcII/CalcII.aspx): Integration techniques, volume, arc length. *(Objectives 0016)*
- [OpenStax: Calculus Volume 1 (free book)](https://openstax.org/details/books/calculus-volume-1): Reference text for limits, derivatives and integrals. *(Objectives 0015, 0016)*
- [OpenStax: Calculus Volume 2 (free book)](https://openstax.org/details/books/calculus-volume-2): Integration techniques and applications. *(Objectives 0016)*
- [3Blue1Brown: Essence of Calculus](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/calculus): Watch first. It gives the big picture in about 3 hours. *(Objectives 0015, 0016)*
- [Levin: Discrete Mathematics, An Open Introduction (free book)](https://discrete.openmathbooks.org/dmoi3/): Logic, sets, counting, induction, graph theory. *(Objectives 0017)*
- [StatLect](https://www.statlect.com/): Probability reference, if you want more depth. *(Objectives 0013)*

## Tools and video teachers

- [Desmos graphing calculator](https://www.desmos.com/calculator): Graph every function you study. It builds intuition fast.
- [Desmos scientific calculator](https://www.desmos.com/scientific): Close to the basic on-screen calculator on the test.
- [Professor Leonard (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@ProfessorLeonard): Full-length lectures: algebra, calculus, statistics.
- [The Organic Chemistry Tutor (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@TheOrganicChemistryTutor): Short videos on almost every single topic. Search by topic name.
- [Anki (flashcard app)](https://apps.ankiweb.net/): Optional. For spaced repetition on your phone in addition to the Flashcards tab.
