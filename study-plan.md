# MTEL Mathematics (63): 8-week study plan

For: rebuilding from algebra, 4–8 weeks, about 12–15 hours each week.
The same plan is in the **Plan** tab of `index.html`, with checkboxes that save your progress.

## The test in one table

| Part | Share of score | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| I. Number Sense and Operations | 10% | 0001 real numbers, 0002 complex numbers, matrices, vectors |
| II. Relations, Functions, and Algebra | **30%** | 0003–0008: equations, functions, lines, quadratics, exp/log, polynomials, rational functions, trig |
| III. Geometry and Measurement | 20% | 0009–0012: units, Euclidean proof, 2D/3D, coordinates, conics, transformations, non-Euclidean |
| IV. Probability, Statistics, Calculus, Discrete | 20% | 0013–0017: probability, statistics, derivatives, integrals, discrete math |
| V. Open response (2 written tasks) | 20% | 0018–0019: combine 2+ topics, model, explain |

85 multiple-choice + 2 open-response questions in 4 hours. Pass = 240 (scaled score). The test gives you an on-screen scientific calculator and a formula sheet.
**Subarea II is the largest part.** Weeks 1–4 build it, because every other subarea uses it.

## Your daily routine (built for visual learners)

Every new topic in the plan says **(Watch → See it → Examples)**. In the app, open Concepts and the topic, then follow these steps:

| Step | Time | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Watch | 5–12 min | One short video from the **Watch** list. Pause and copy one picture or graph into your notebook. |
| 2. See it | 5 min | Move the sliders in the **See it** visual. Predict what will happen before you move a slider, then check. |
| 3. Examples | 15–20 min | Click "Show next step" on all 3 worked examples. Try to say the next step before you reveal it. Each starts with "Picture it:"; sketch that picture first. |
| 4. Drill | 20–30 min | Practice questions on the topic. Sketch a quick graph or diagram for every question, even if it seems easy. |
| 5. Cards | 10 min | Flashcards. |

## How to study (the method)

1. **Read, then close the book.** Read the Concepts card. Then write the key ideas from memory. Recall (pulling it out of your head) builds memory much better than rereading.
2. **Worked example, then try alone.** Follow the example one step at a time ("Show next step"). Then do a drill question without looking.
3. **Error log.** For every miss, write: the question, what you did wrong, and the correct method. Redo the log every Sunday.
4. **Flashcards every day, 10 minutes.** The app uses boxes: a card you know moves up and comes back less often. A card you miss goes back to box 1.
5. **Mix topics.** From week 2, end each Saturday with a mixed drill. The real test mixes topics, so practice switching between them.
6. **Explain it aloud.** If you cannot explain a step to a 10-year-old, you do not own it yet. Note it in the log.
7. **Graph everything** on Desmos. Seeing a function makes its algebra easier to remember.
8. **Draw before you compute.** On the test, a 10-second sketch (a number line, a graph, a triangle) catches most sign and setup errors.

## If your test is in 4–6 weeks (triage track)

Combine two weeks into one, and drop the lowest-value details on the first pass:

| Week | Do | Skip on the first pass |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plan weeks 1 + 2 | polar and De Moivre (come back later) |
| 2 | Plan weeks 3 + 4 | partial fractions, constructions |
| 3 | Plan weeks 5 + 6 | non-Euclidean detail, shell method, arc length, integration by parts |
| 4 | Official practice test on day 1, fix weak areas, in-app full mock on day 5 | nothing new |

## Test-day pacing

- 240 minutes total. Plan about **2 minutes per multiple-choice question** (85 × 2 = 170 min) and **about 30 minutes per open response** (60 min). That leaves 10 minutes spare.
- First pass: answer what you can. Flag any question that takes more than 3 minutes, guess, and move on. There is no penalty for a wrong answer, so never leave a blank.
- Start the open responses with **at least 60 minutes left**. Some people do them first, while they are fresh. Choose one approach and use it on your practice tests.
- Open response: answer every bullet in order and label each part. Show every step. Write the final answer in a sentence with units.
- If you draw a graph by hand, leave about 5 minutes to scan it in. Watch the official [hand-drawing tutorial](https://www.mtel.nesinc.com/PageView.aspx?f=HTML_FRAG/GENRB_CBTTutorials.html) before test day.
